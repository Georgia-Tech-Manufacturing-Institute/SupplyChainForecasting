Performance Indicators

The performance columns below compare each model's accuracy on its holdout set against a naive baseline:

  • R² (R-squared) — Measure of model "fit", with 1 being a perfect fit; A good model has an R2 greater than the waterfall R2 but less than 1.
  • MAE (Mean Absolute Error) — average absolute error between the model prediction and consumed quantity. Lower is better.
  • MSE (Mean Squared Error) — average squared error between the model prediction and consumed quantity; penalizes large misses more heavily than MAE. Lower is better.

Model and Waterfall columns utilize the Validation weeks to provide model performance estimates. If no validation weeks were set at the time of training, these cells will be empty. Model columns give the fit quality of the ML model on the validation weeks Waterfall columns score a naive baseline that simply carries forward the most recent known waterfall value. Green means the model beat that baseline; red means it underperformed.
